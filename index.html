Why We Built This

We Know Both Sides of This Journey.

John David was born and raised in California — but he grew up in an immigrant family. He saw sacrifice up close, including seasons where stability was not guaranteed. That experience didn't break him. It shaped how he sees every family that walks through our door.

Wellen came from Brazil. She is the immigrant. She knows what it feels like to navigate a system in a language that wasn't yours, to miss home while trying to build a new one, to want to protect your family but not know the right moves.

Together, we built Prosper In America because we have lived both sides of this story. We know there is a better path — not just to survive, but to build something real, something lasting, something your family can stand on for generations.

Faith is the foundation. Family is the mission. You are the reason.
