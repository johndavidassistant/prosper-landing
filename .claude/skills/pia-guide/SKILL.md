# /pia-guide

Create, edit, or structure content for the Prosper In America Starter Guide — the free lead magnet delivered after Tally form submission.

## Trigger

- `/pia-guide outline` — generate or review the 7-step guide outline
- `/pia-guide step [N]` — draft or rewrite a specific step (1–7)
- `/pia-guide intro` — write the guide introduction
- `/pia-guide brief` — output a Canva design brief for the PDF layout
- `/pia-guide status` — check current state: what's in the vault, what's missing, what's blocking delivery

## Guide identity

**Title (EN):** "7 Steps to Building Your Life in America — The Brazilian Immigrant's Starter Guide"
**Title (PT):** "Os 7 Passos Para Construir Sua Vida Nos EUA — O Guia Inicial do Imigrante Brasileiro"

**Format target:** 12–16 page PDF, designed in Canva, matching PIA brand (navy/gold/cream, Montserrat/Inter)

**Purpose:** Lead magnet. Captures email via Tally form (`q4E9x7`). Delivered via ConvertKit email sequence (not yet built). Must be valuable enough standalone that the reader wants to book a call after finishing it.

**Tone:** Same as the landing page — warm authority. A friend who knows the system, not a bureaucrat explaining it.

## The 7 steps

Source of truth in vault: `~/Documents/OBSIDIAN/REINO DE DEUS/06 Prosper In America/`

Before writing any step, read the relevant vault file for that topic. Do not invent legal or immigration information.

1. **Understand Your Status** — know your current legal standing before making any move
2. **Build Your Credit Foundation** — ITIN, secured card, credit history from zero
3. **Get Your Documents Right** — ITIN, SSN eligibility, notarized docs, translations
4. **Open Your Financial Accounts** — bank account options without SSN, remittances, savings
5. **Protect Your Family** — life insurance options for immigrants, ITIN-based policies
6. **Navigate the Legal Path** — when to consult an attorney, what to avoid, timelines
7. **Build Your Network** — church, community, Brazilian professionals, accountability
