Decision call

What changed from V1

  • Custom-drawn paths — no font, no rendering drift. Same look on every machine.
  • Stem width raised to 35% of letter width — V1 was Saira-thin. V2 is Druk-Wide-heavy.
  • Removed gold dot — was decoration, not structure. Gone.
  • Variant A: no frame. Pure letterform discipline. Bugatti EB / YSL territory.
  • Variant B: 24px frame. Stamped, sealed, intentional. No hairline.
  • 64px legibility: both pass. Bars stay solid, mouth of G stays open, B bowls stay clear.
