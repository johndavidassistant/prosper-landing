5 — Decisions baked into this V1

  • Palette: matte black + gold #C8952E (shared with PIA) + cream/white
  • Type: heavy extended sans — Druk Wide / Tungsten Wide preferred, Saira (Google Font, free) as fallback
  • Period in gold: recurring motif. Connects monogram and wordmark.
  • Thin gold frame on monogram: minimal geometric structure (Q6 secondary).
  • Tagline restraint: "THE GO BIG AGENCY" tracked very tight, never shouted.

6 — How to use these files

For IG profile picture: right-click logo-monogram.svg → "Open With Preview" → Export as PNG, 1080×1080. Upload.

For WhatsApp Business avatar: same PNG. WA crops to circle — the monogram still reads.

For email signature: use logo-wordmark.svg at 240px wide.

For proposal cover: see mock above. logo-wordmark.svg top-left.

If the type does not feel right: install Druk Wide or Tungsten Wide on your Mac and reload this page. Premium fonts will render automatically (font stack falls through).
