What changed from V2

Anchored to real-world brand standards

  • Out: hand-drawn rect-stencil shapes (V2 read as Atari, you called it).
  • In: Helvetica Neue Black — real grotesk letterforms with proper bowls, counters, and notch on the G.
  • Tesla move (Variant A): 1.18× horizontal scale gives the wide-and-confident proportion without licensing Druk Wide. Letters almost touch. No decoration whatsoever.
  • Balenciaga move (Variant B): kerning at -0.06em — extreme tight, all-caps discipline.
  • Off-White move (Variant B): two thin gold horizontal rules with tracked uppercase metadata above and below. Industrial-luxury notation. Not decorative — informational.
  • What is the same as V2: matte black ground, gold #C8952E (shared with PIA), white type, 64px legibility.
