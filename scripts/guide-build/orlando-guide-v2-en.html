One

The Springs

The Florida nobody plans for, and the Florida everyone remembers.

Blue Springs State Park
35 min north · $6 per car
72°F all year. Manatees from November to March, dozens at a time from a boardwalk. Get there by 9 AM in winter or it fills up.
De Leon Springs
45 min north · $6 per car
Flip your own pancakes at the Old Spanish Sugar Mill, then swim in the spring next door. A real Florida day.
Rock Springs at Kelly Park
40 min north · entry fee + tube rental
Rent a tube and float a one-mile spring-fed run that ends in a swimming hole. Pure Florida. Get there before 11 AM on weekends or they cap entries.
Two

Beaches Worth
the Drive

Two coasts to choose from. Both about an hour out.

New Smyrna Beach
1 hr east · East Coast
Less crowded than Daytona. You can drive on the beach. Best swim and sunset combo on the east coast.
Cocoa Beach
45 min east · East Coast
Surf rentals, the famous Ron Jon shop, classic Florida boardwalk feel. Good for a half-day.
Clearwater Beach
1.5 hr west · Gulf Coast
White sand, calmer water, gulf side. If your visit lines up with sunset, drive west.
Three

Towns to Wander

Walking towns. Lake views. A different pace than the parks.

Mount Dora
45 min northwest · free
Charming small town on a lake. Antique shops, lakefront dining, lake cruise if you like.
Winter Park
15 min from downtown · free
Right inside Orlando but feels like its own world. Park Avenue shops, brick streets, the Morse Museum if you like Tiffany glass.
Winter Garden
30 min west · Saturday is the move
The Saturday morning farmers market is the move. Park downtown, walk the brick streets, grab a coffee, watch families ride the West Orange Trail.
Four

Brazilian Comfort

For when you miss home.

Seabra Foods
Grocery · butcher · pay-by-the-pound buffet
Brazilian grocery, butcher, and pay-by-the-pound hot lunch buffet — my favorite, weigh and eat. Real cuts for churrasco, products straight from Brazil. The community spot when you miss home.
Camilla's
Brazilian restaurant
Feijoada on weekends, picanha any day, Brazilian breakfast. Authentic, warm, a favorite for families.
First Orlando Church — Brazilian Campus
Sundays 9 AM · free breakfast before service
Brazilian Sunday service. Arrive at 9 AM for free breakfast before the service. A real community welcome.
God of Promises Church
Sunday evenings 6 PM
Smaller, intimate, vision-driven. If you're looking for a church, both of these are home to me.
Five

Theme Parks,
the Honest Take

Most of what you read is sponsored. Here's what I'd actually tell a friend.

Magic Kingdom (Disney)
Worth it once
Get there at park-open, hit the headliners before 11 AM, take a midday break at the hotel pool, come back for the fireworks. One day, not three.
Universal Studios + Islands of Adventure
Better for adults and older kids
Better park experience than Disney for adults and older kids. Harry Potter is the reason. Buy the Express Pass if your budget allows — saves 4-5 hours of waiting.
Volcano Bay
Best water park in Orlando
The best water park in Orlando. Better than Disney's. Half-day in summer.
Disney Springs
Skip the "park" framing
It's a shopping and restaurant complex, not an experience. Worth a 2-hour evening visit for a meal and a wander, not a destination.
Six

Where to Stay

If you have flexibility on lodging, this matters more than you think.

Windsor Island Resort
Davenport · 25 min from the parks
Where I stay when family visits. Gated, high security, full resort with a water park, lazy river, gym, restaurant. Quieter than the Disney hotels and roughly half the price for what you get.
Seven

Practical

The stuff a guide usually skips.

Driving
Orlando is car-dependent. Toll roads everywhere. Get a SunPass or pay the rental car's toll plan. Google Maps underestimates traffic between 4 PM and 7 PM. Add 30%.
Weather
Summer rain comes daily at 3 PM, lasts 30 minutes. Don't cancel your plans. Just get under cover for a beat.
Tipping
20% standard at restaurants. $2-5 per bag at hotels. Rideshare: anything is appreciated, nothing is required.
Language
Most of the service industry in Orlando speaks Spanish and a fair amount of Portuguese. Brazilian restaurants always speak Portuguese. Spanish or English will get you anywhere.
Common scams
Three to watch for: anyone selling "discount tickets" near park gates — walk away. Timeshare presentations promising free park tickets — your morning vanishes for tickets you could have bought online for less. Rideshare drivers quoting flat rates off-app — always book in-app.
